The watch

The watch runs on its own, from boot, in the oasis-nwr service. Start it, stop it or give it a dongle in Setup. This page reads it and listens in.

Spoken alerts

Off until you switch it on: a watch that starts talking the moment you assign a dongle is a watch you turn off. On, an alert on your county list is spoken aloud through the station voice. There is no severity filter, on purpose - you will also hear the Required Weekly Test, and that weekly announcement is the only regular proof that demodulation, parsing, matching and speech all still work.

Your counties

The watch list is how you say which counties matter to you. It decides what is flagged in the log, what the dashboard card counts as yours, and what the bell speaks - not what is decoded; everything heard is recorded either way. An empty list matches everything, which is the safe default; a wrong list matches nothing and looks exactly like a dead receiver.
