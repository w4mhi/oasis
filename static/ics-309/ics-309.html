Communications Log

BlockTitleInstructions
1Incident NameEnter the name assigned to the incident.
2Operational PeriodEnter the start and end date (month/day/year) and time (24-hour clock) for the operational period to which the form applies.
3Radio Net NameEnter the name or designator of the radio net being logged.
4Radio OperatorEnter the name and call sign of the radio operator maintaining this log.
5SignatureSignature of the radio operator.
LogTimeEnter the time (24-hour clock) of the message or activity being logged.
LogMessageEnter the content of the message or a brief description of the activity.
LogFrom — Call Sign / IDEnter the call sign or identifier of the station originating the message.
LogFrom Msg #Enter the message number assigned by the originating station, if applicable.
LogTo — Call Sign / IDEnter the call sign or identifier of the receiving station.
LogTo Msg #Enter the message number assigned by the receiving station, if applicable.
6Prepared ByEnter the name and ICS position of the person preparing the form.
7Date & Time PreparedEnter date (month/day/year) and time prepared (24-hour clock).

Purpose: Records all radio communications activity for a given net and operational period. Provides a complete record for the Documentation Unit and supports after-action review.

Distribution: Completed ICS 309s are submitted to the Communications Unit Leader and forwarded to the Documentation Unit. Operators should retain a copy.
