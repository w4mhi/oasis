Q-Codes

Standard operating codes used on amateur and HF radio

CodeMeaning (statement)Meaning (question)Notes
QRMI am being interfered withAre you being interfered with?Man-made interference — other stations, noise from equipment
QRNI am troubled by staticAre you troubled by static?Natural interference — lightning, atmospheric noise
QROIncrease transmitter powerShall I increase power?Opposite of QRP
QRPDecrease transmitter powerShall I decrease power?Commonly used to mean low-power operation (<5W)
QRSSend more slowlyShall I send more slowly?Primarily CW; sometimes used on digital modes
QRTI am closing downShall I stop sending?Station going off the air
QRUI have nothing for youHave you anything for me?Common on traffic nets — no traffic to pass
QRVI am readyAre you ready?EmComm Used to confirm readiness to receive traffic
QRXI will call you again at… hrsWhen will you call me again?Standby / wait
QRZYou are being called by…Who is calling me?Common at end of CQ or after unclear callsign
QSBYour signals are fadingAre my signals fading?Signal strength variation — multipath or ionospheric
QSLI acknowledge receiptCan you acknowledge receipt?EmComm Confirm message received and understood
QSOI can communicate with…Can you communicate with…?A two-way contact or conversation
QSPI will relay to…Will you relay to…?EmComm Traffic relay through a third station
QSTGeneral call to all stations—Bulletin or announcement intended for all
QSYChange to frequency…Shall I change frequency?Move to another frequency — specify in kHz or MHz
QTCI have… messages for youHow many messages do you have?EmComm Used on traffic nets to indicate message count
QTHMy location is…What is your location?Give city, grid square, or address as appropriate
QTRThe exact time is…What is the exact time?Always give time in UTC (Zulu) during activations
QTXI will keep my station openWill you keep your station open?EmComm Station remains available for further traffic
