Procedure Words (Pro-words)

Standard voice procedure terms for clear, unambiguous radio communication

Pro-wordMeaningNotes
AFFIRMATIVEYes / that is correctnot "Roger" Use when confirming a fact, not just acknowledging receipt
ALL AFTERThe portion of the message after the word I have specifiedUsed when asking for a repeat of part of a message
ALL BEFOREThe portion of the message before the word I have specifiedUsed when asking for a repeat of part of a message
BREAKI am separating the text from other portions of the messageEmComm Pause between header and body of a formal message
CORRECTYou are correct / what you have transmitted is correctConfirms accuracy of information, not just receipt
DISREGARDIgnore my last transmissionNot "Cancel" — used when an error was made
GO AHEADProceed with your transmissionPermission to transmit; response to a station calling you
I SAY AGAINI am repeating my last transmissionPreferred over "I repeat" — "repeat" is an artillery term
NEGATIVENo / that is not correct / I cannot / I do notUse instead of "no" for clarity over noisy channels
OUTThis exchange is ended; no reply expectednever "Over and Out" Over = reply expected; Out = conversation ended
OVERMy transmission is ended; your reply is expectedPass the channel to the other station
READ BACKRepeat this entire transmission back to me exactly as receivedEmComm Used to verify critical information
ROGERI have received and understood your last transmissionnot "yes" Does not mean agreement — only receipt and understanding
SAY AGAINRepeat your last transmissionPreferred over "repeat" for the reason above
STANDBYI must pause for a few seconds; do not call meIf longer than a few seconds, give an estimated return time
WILCOI have received your message, understand it, and will complyImplies both Roger and intent to act — do not say "Roger Wilco"
