RST Signal Reporting

Readability · Signal Strength · Tone (CW/digital)

RST = Readability + Signal Strength + Tone (CW/digital only). Voice reports use RS only (e.g. "59"). A "5-9" report means perfectly readable, extremely strong signal. Give honest reports — an inflated "59" is useless. On digital modes, report what you actually see in the waterfall.

S9 = maximum on most meter scales (~50µV at antenna)
S9+ reports (S9+10dB, S9+20dB, etc.) indicate signals above S9 — common on busy HF bands.
Typical voice exchange: "You are 5 by 7" or "59 here" — Readability first, then Strength.
EmComm note: For formal message traffic, a signal report is not required but helps the receiving operator assess path quality.
