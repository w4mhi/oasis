ICS 213 — General Message
7. Message
8. Approved By
9. Reply
10. Replied By
BlockTitleInstructions
1Incident NameOptional. Enter the name assigned to the incident.
2ToEnter the name and position the message is intended for. Use at least first initial and last name. For Unified Command, include agency names.
3FromEnter the name and position of the individual sending the message. Use at least first initial and last name. For Unified Command, include agency names.
4SubjectEnter the subject of the message.
5DateEnter the date (month/day/year) of the message.
6TimeEnter the time (24-hour clock) of the message.
7MessageEnter the content of the message. Be as concise as possible.
8Approved ByEnter the name, signature, and ICS position/title of the person approving the message.
9ReplyThe intended recipient enters a reply to the message and returns it to the originator.
10Replied ByEnter the name, ICS position/title, and signature of the person replying. Enter date (month/day/year) and time prepared (24-hour clock).

Purpose: Used to record incoming messages that cannot be orally transmitted, and to transmit messages (resource orders, incident name changes, ICS coordination) via radio or telephone. Used for any message requiring hard-copy delivery.

Notes: Traditionally a three-part carbon form — sender completes the message and sends parts 2 and 3 to the recipient; recipient completes the reply and returns part 3 to the sender. A copy must be sent to the Documentation Unit.
