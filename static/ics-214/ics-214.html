BlockTitleInstructions
1Incident NameEnter the name assigned to the incident.
2Operational PeriodEnter the start date (month/day/year) and time (24-hour clock) and end date and time for the operational period. Example: Date 11/14/2012, Time 0700.
3NameEnter the title of the organizational unit or resource designator (e.g., Facilities Unit, Safety Officer, Strike Team).
4ICS PositionEnter the name and ICS position of the individual in charge of the unit.
5Home Agency (and Unit)Enter the home agency of the individual completing the ICS 214. Enter a unit designator if utilized by the jurisdiction or discipline.
6Resources AssignedEnter name, ICS position, and home agency/unit for each assigned resource. For individuals, use at least first initial and last name. Cell phone number may be added optionally.
7Activity LogEnter the time (24-hour clock) and briefly describe individual notable activities. Note the date if the operational period covers more than one day. Activities may include task assignments, completions, injuries, difficulties encountered, etc.
8Prepared ByEnter the name, ICS position/title, and signature of the person preparing the form. Enter date (month/day/year) and time prepared (24-hour clock).

Purpose: Records details of notable activities at any ICS level, including single resources, equipment, Task Forces, etc. Provides basic incident activity documentation and a reference for after-action reports.

Distribution: Completed ICS 214s are submitted to supervisors, who forward to the Documentation Unit. All completed originals must be given to the Documentation Unit. Individuals should retain a copy for their own records.

Notes: The ICS 214 can be printed as a two-sided form. Use additional copies as continuation sheets as needed.
